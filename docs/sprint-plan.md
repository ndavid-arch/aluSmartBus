# Sprint Plan

The project follows **Scrum** (SRS Section 2). I am both the Product Owner and the developer. A classmate or my supervisor reviews each sprint, and ALU riders and drivers give feedback.

Each sprint ends with a working piece to review, and a short retrospective noted at the bottom of this file.

| Sprint | Goal | Main requirements | Who tests it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sprint 0** | Set up the repo and board, prototype the screens in Figma, scaffold the backend and front end, test background GPS on a phone | — | Classmates (prototype) |
| **Sprint 1** (weeks 1–2) | Log-in and roles, register buses, drivers start a journey | FR-01 to FR-08, FR-13 to FR-17, FR-36, FR-37, FR-49 to FR-52 | Drivers |
| **Sprint 2** (weeks 3–4) | Share the bus location, show the map and stops, show arrival times | FR-09, FR-18 to FR-27 | Me, riding the bus |
| **Sprint 3** (weeks 5–6) | Students choose a stop, detect arrival, send alerts | FR-10 to FR-12, FR-28 to FR-35, FR-44 to FR-48 | Students |
| **Sprint 4** (weeks 7–8) | QR boarding, Full status, admin reports | FR-38 to FR-43, FR-53 to FR-59 | ALU transport staff |

## Labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `user story` | A feature written from the user's point of view |
| `task` | Technical or set-up work |
| `spike` | A short experiment to reduce a risk |
| `bug` | Something that does not work as specified |
| `area: …` | The component the work belongs to (matches the component diagram) |
| `role: …` | The user who benefits |
| `priority: high / medium / low` | Order of work inside a sprint |
